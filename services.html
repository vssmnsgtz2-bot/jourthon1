05 — Regulatory & Compliance

Navigate Regulations with Confidence

Health Canada. FDA-oriented review. Export readiness — coordinated with a compliance-first mindset.

Regulatory compliance is one of the most complex parts of bringing a health supplement to market. Jourthon helps organize the pathway, label review, product documentation, and submission-related preparation for North American and selected global market needs.

  • NPN Natural Product Number application coordination
  • DIN registration support, where applicable
  • FDA DSHEA compliance review support
  • Product licence applications and renewals coordination
  • Site license coordination
  • Export compliance for EU, Australia, and Asia-Pacific planning
  • Post-market documentation and reporting process support
