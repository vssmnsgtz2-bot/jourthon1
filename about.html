About Jourthon

Rooted in North America. Built for Supplement Brands and Private-Label Clients.

Jourthon Nutrition Inc. was founded in Canada to help health supplement companies navigate the full path from concept to compliant production and supply readiness.

Our Story

A full-chain manufacturing partner for supplement companies

Jourthon was created for brands that need more than a factory contact or a single consultant. Successful supplement manufacturing programs require formula decisions, packaging choices, regulatory documentation, supplier coordination, quality control, and market execution to move together.

By connecting scientific formulation support, North American compliance awareness, manufacturing resources, packaging coordination, and production and supply readiness planning, Jourthon gives clients a more coherent route from idea to shelf.
